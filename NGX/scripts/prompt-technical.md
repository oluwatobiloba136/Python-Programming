## 1. Core Principles & Approach
- **Understand the foundational premise**: Price discounts all information, history tends to repeat, and prices move in trends.
- **Select appropriate timeframe**: Match your trading style (day trading: intraday charts; swing trading: daily/weekly; position trading: weekly/monthly).
- **Choose chart type**: Candlestick (preferred for price action), OHLC bar, line, or Heikin-Ashi based on analysis needs.
- **Establish the primary trend**: Use higher timeframes to determine the dominant direction before analyzing lower timeframes for entries.



## 2. Trend Analysis
- **Identify trend direction**: Higher highs and higher lows (uptrend), lower highs and lower lows (downtrend), or sideways movement (range-bound).
- **Draw trendlines**: Connect at least two swing lows for uptrend support or two swing highs for downtrend resistance.
- **Use moving averages**: 
  - Short-term: 9, 20, 50-period for momentum
  - Medium-term: 100, 200-period for trend direction
  - Observe crossovers (golden cross/death cross) and price relative to MA
- **Assess trend strength**: ADX (Average Directional Index) readings above 25 indicate strong trend; below 20 suggests weak/non-trending market.

## 3. Support and Resistance Levels
- **Horizontal levels**: Identify price zones where the stock has repeatedly reversed (pivot points, previous highs/lows).
- **Dynamic support/resistance**: Use moving averages, Fibonacci retracements (38.2%, 50%, 61.8%), or pivot point calculations.
- **Psychological levels**: Round numbers (e.g., $50, $100) often act as support/resistance.
- **Volume confirmation**: Valid breakouts/breakdowns should occur with above-average volume.

## 4. Key Technical Indicators
- **Momentum Oscillators**:
  - RSI (Relative Strength Index): Overbought >70, oversold <30; watch for divergences
  - Stochastic: Similar to RSI but more sensitive in ranging markets
  - CCI (Commodity Channel Index): Identifies cyclical trends
- **Trend-Following Indicators**:
  - MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence): Signal line crossovers, histogram, zero-line crosses
  - Parabolic SAR: Stop-and-reverse points for trend following
  - Ichimoku Cloud: Comprehensive system showing support/resistance, momentum, and trend direction
- **Volatility Indicators**:
  - Bollinger Bands: Price relative to 2-standard deviation bands; squeeze indicates low volatility
  - ATR (Average True Range): Measures volatility for stop-loss placement
  - Keltner Channels: Similar to Bollinger Bands but uses ATR

## 5. Volume Analysis
- **Confirm price moves**: Rising prices should accompany rising volume; falling prices on high volume suggest strong selling pressure.
- **Volume indicators**:
  - OBV (On-Balance Volume): Cumulative volume flow
  - VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price): Intraday benchmark for institutional activity
  - Volume-weighted moving averages
- **Volume climaxes**: Extreme volume spikes often signal exhaustion points.

## 6. Chart Patterns
- **Continuation patterns**: Flags, pennants, triangles (ascending/descending/symmetrical), rectangles.
- **Reversal patterns**: 
  - Head and shoulders (top/bottom), double/triple tops/bottoms
  - Wedges (rising/falling)
  - Cup and handle
- **Candlestick patterns**:
  - Single candle: Doji, hammer, shooting star, spinning top
  - Multiple candle: Engulfing patterns, harami, morning/evening star, three white soldiers/black crows

## 7. Trading Strategy Framework
- **Entry signals**: 
  - Trend-following: Buy pullbacks to support in uptrend, sell rallies to resistance in downtrend
  - Breakout: Enter on confirmed break of resistance/support with volume
  - Reversal: Enter at key support/resistance with oscillator divergence and candlestick confirmation
- **Exit signals**:
  - Profit targets: Previous swing points, measured move from patterns, Fibonacci extensions
  - Stop-loss placement: Below recent swing low (for longs) or above recent swing high (for shorts); use ATR for volatility-adjusted stops
  - Trailing stops: Use moving averages, parabolic SAR, or percentage-based trails
- **Risk management**: 
  - Position size based on risk tolerance (e.g., 1-2% of capital per trade)
  - Risk-reward ratio minimum of 1:2
  - Maximum daily/weekly loss limits

## 8. Multiple Timeframe Analysis
- **Higher timeframe (HTF)**: Determine primary trend and key support/resistance zones (weekly for swing traders, monthly for position traders).
- **Middle timeframe (MTF)**: Identify trade setup and entry triggers (daily for swing traders).
- **Lower timeframe (LTF)**: Fine-tune entry timing and check for micro-patterns (hourly/15-min for day traders).
- **Ensure alignment**: Trades have higher probability when all timeframes show confluence.

## 9. Market Context & Confirmation
- **Sector strength**: Check if the stock is moving with or against its sector peers.
- **Market correlation**: Assess relationship to broader market indices (e.g. NGX All Share).
- **Seasonality**: Consider historical monthly/quarterly tendencies.
- **News/events**: Be aware of scheduled earnings, economic releases, or corporate events that could override technicals.

## 10. Technical Analysis Checklist (Pre-Trade)
- [ ] Trend direction confirmed on HTF and MTF
- [ ] Key support/resistance levels identified
- [ ] Momentum oscillator not at extreme without divergence
- [ ] Volume confirms price action
- [ ] Clear chart pattern or candlestick signal present
- [ ] Entry, stop-loss, and target levels defined
- [ ] Risk-reward ratio meets minimum threshold
- [ ] No major conflicting signals on LTF
- [ ] Aligns with fundamental outlook (if using combined approach)
