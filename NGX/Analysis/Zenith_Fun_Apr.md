- **Zenith Bank Plc** is a **Tier‑1 Nigerian commercial bank** offering **retail, SME, corporate and institutional banking** services: current/savings accounts, term deposits, loans & advances, trade finance, treasury, e‑channels, agency banking, and other financial services.

  - **Interest income** from **loans**, **placements**, and **investment securities**.  
  - **Non‑interest income** from **fees & commissions**, **electronic banking fees**, **trading and FX income**, and **other operating income**.  
  - See FY‑2025 results:

## 8. Glossary of Abbreviations & What They Indicate

- **NGX** – Nigerian Exchange Group (main stock exchange in Nigeria).  
- **NII (Net Interest Income)** – interest income minus interest expense; core banking earnings from lending and investments.  
- **NIR (Non‑Interest Revenue / Income)** – income from fees, commissions, FX trading, etc.; diversifies earnings but can be volatile.  
- **PBT (Profit Before Tax)** – earnings before tax; indicates core profitability before tax.  
- **PAT (Profit After Tax)** – net income after tax; drives EPS and dividend capacity.  
- **EPS (Earnings Per Share)** – PAT divided by number of shares; key indicator of per‑share profitability.  
- **ROE (Return on Equity)** – PAT / average shareholders’ equity; >20% is very strong for banks.  
- **ROA (Return on Assets)** – PAT / average total assets; >2–3% is strong for banks.  
- **CIR (Cost‑to‑Income Ratio)** – operating expenses / operating income; lower is better (Zenith ~45%).  
- **CAR (Capital Adequacy Ratio)** – regulatory capital / risk‑weighted assets; higher values mean more cushion against losses.  
- **P/E (Price‑to‑Earnings Ratio)** – share price / EPS; low P/E with strong earnings suggests undervaluation.  
- **P/B (Price‑to‑Book Ratio)** – share price / book value per share; <1× often implies market values equity below net assets.  
- **P/TBV (Price‑to‑Tangible Book Value)** – price / (equity minus intangibles); conservative book value metric.  
- **DPS (Dividend Per Share)** – cash dividend per share; important for income investors.  
- **Dividend Yield** – DPS / current price; Zenith’s forward yield ~9–11% is high.  
- **CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate)** – smoothed average growth rate over multiple periods.  
- **CBN (Central Bank of Nigeria)** – banking sector regulator.  
- **NCCG (Nigerian Code of Corporate Governance)** – corporate governance framework in Nigeria.
