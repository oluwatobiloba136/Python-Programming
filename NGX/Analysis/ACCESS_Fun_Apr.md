## 1. Business Model & Qualitative Overview

### 1.1 Core Business & Revenue Model

- **Access Holdings Plc (ACCESSCORP)** is a **financial holding company** that owns **Access Bank Plc** and non‑bank subsidiaries in **payments, consumer lending, insurance brokerage and pension/asset management**.  
  - Investor relations hub:  
    - https://theaccesscorporation.com/investor-relations/  
  - Corporate disclosure page:  
    - https://accessholdingsplc.com/investor-relations/corporate-disclosure/  

- The group’s revenues are **dominated by banking** via Access Bank:  
  - **Interest income** on loans, placements and investment securities.  
  - **Non‑interest income** from fees & commissions, digital channels, trading and FX income.  
  - Income statement detail:  
    - https://stockanalysis.com/quote/ngx/ACCESSCORP/financials/  

- The HoldCo earns via **dividends and management fees** from subsidiaries and is building earnings from **non‑bank verticals** (payments, consumer lending, insurance).  

- Revenues are partly **recurring** (retail deposits/transaction banking) but also **cyclical** with Nigerian and African macro, interest rates and FX.  

- Customer base spans **retail, SMEs, corporates and public sector** across Nigeria and >15 African markets plus the UK.  
  - Market profile & annual reports list:  
    - https://www.african-markets.com/en/stock-markets/ngse/listed-companies/company?code=ACCESSCORP  

### 1.2 Industry & Competitive Landscape

- ACCESSCORP is one of Nigeria’s largest banking groups by assets, alongside **Zenith Bank, GTCO, UBA, FBNH**.  
- It has aggressively expanded via acquisition into **Zambia, Botswana, South Africa (Grobank & SCB assets), Angola, Mozambique, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania**, etc.  
  - Strategic expansion & acquisition releases (examples within 2023–25):  
    - Example: proposed acquisition of Standard Chartered businesses & other African banks referenced in NGX corporate disclosures:

## 11. Glossary of Abbreviations & What They Indicate

- **NGX** – Nigerian Exchange; main stock market. Listing here provides liquidity and regulatory oversight.  
- **HoldCo (Holding Company)** – Parent entity owning multiple subsidiaries; allows diversification but adds complexity.  
- **ROE (Return on Equity)** – Net income / shareholders’ equity; measures how effectively equity capital is deployed. >20% is strong for banks.  
- **ROA (Return on Assets)** – Net income / total assets; gauges efficiency of asset use. Lower ROA suggests less efficient balance sheet.  
- **P/E (Price‑to‑Earnings)** – Share price / EPS; low P/E with solid earnings suggests undervaluation.  
- **P/B (Price‑to‑Book)** – Share price / book value per share; <1× means market values the company below net asset value.  
- **P/TBV (Price‑to‑Tangible Book Value)** – Price / tangible book (equity minus intangibles); stricter measure of balance sheet cheapness.  
- **PS (Price‑to‑Sales)** – Price / revenue; low PS with good margins can be attractive.  
- **D/E (Debt‑to‑Equity)** – Total debt / equity; measures leverage. High D/E amplifies both returns and risk.  
- **EPS (Earnings Per Share)** – Net income / shares outstanding; key driver of long‑term price and dividends.  
- **DPS (Dividend Per Share)** – Annual cash dividend per share; key for income‑focused investors.  
- **Dividend Yield** – DPS / share price; indicates percentage of price returned as cash annually.  
- **Payout Ratio** – DPS / EPS; low ratio (<30%) suggests room for dividend growth and retained earnings.  
- **NII (Net Interest Income)** – Interest income minus interest expense; core banking profitability measure.  
- **NPL (Non‑Performing Loan)** – Loan in default or close to it; high NPLs indicate credit‑risk issues.  
- **CET1 (Common Equity Tier 1)** – Core capital measure used by regulators; higher CET1 means stronger solvency.  
- **DCF (Discounted Cash Flow)** – Valuation method that discounts future cash flows to present value.  
- **DDM (Dividend Discount Model)** – DCF method focused on dividends.  
- **g (Growth rate)** – Assumed annual growth in EPS or DPS; key DCF/DDM input.  
- **k (Cost of Equity)** – Required return for equity investors; used as discount rate.
