## Glossary of Technical Abbreviations & What They Indicate

- **HTF (Higher Timeframe)** – Weekly/monthly charts; define the **primary trend** and big picture context.  
- **MTF (Middle Timeframe)** – Daily charts; used for trade setup and swing entries.  
- **LTF (Lower Timeframe)** – 4h/1h/15m charts; used for precise timing and micro‑patterns.  
- **MA (Moving Average)** – Average price over N periods (e.g. 20, 50, 100, 200); acts as **dynamic support/resistance** and trend filter.  
- **EMA (Exponential Moving Average)** – Moving average that **weights recent prices more**; reacts faster than simple MA.  
- **RSI (Relative Strength Index)** – Momentum oscillator (0–100);  
  - >70 = **overbought** (risk of pullback),  
  - <30 = **oversold** (potential bounce),  
  - 40–60 = **neutral**; used for trend strength and divergence.  
- **MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)** – Trend/momentum indicator derived from EMAs;  
  - Line above zero = bullish bias,  
  - Crosses & histogram show **momentum shifts**.  
- **ADX (Average Directional Index)** – Measures **trend strength** (0–100);  
  - <20 = weak/no trend,  
  - >25 = trending market.  
- **ATR (Average True Range)** – Measures **volatility** (average range); higher ATR means **wider moves** and suggests **wider stops**.  
- **SAR (Parabolic SAR)** – Trend‑following “stop and reverse” dots above/below price; useful for **trailing stops**.  
- **BB / Bollinger Bands** – Volatility bands (±2 standard deviations around a MA);  
  - Touching upper band = strong upward momentum / overextension,  
  - “BB squeeze” = low volatility → potential breakout.  
- **S/R (Support/Resistance)** – Price levels/zones where price historically **stops or reverses**; important for entries, exits, and stops.  
- **ATH (All‑Time High)** – Highest price ever traded; often a **strong resistance** and breakout reference.  
- **R:R (Risk‑Reward Ratio)** – Reward per unit of risk (e.g. 2:1); higher R:R means **better trade economics**.  
- **TP (Take Profit)** – Predefined price level to close a trade in profit.  
- **SL (Stop‑Loss)** – Predefined level to cut a losing trade; key for **risk management**.  
- **Divergence** – When price and indicators (RSI/MACD) move in **opposite directions** (e.g. price makes higher high but RSI makes lower high); can signal **trend weakening** or potential reversal.
