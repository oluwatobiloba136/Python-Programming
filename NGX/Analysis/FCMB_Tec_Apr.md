### 7.2 For a Swing Trader (days–weeks)

#### 0–3 Days (Immediate)

- With price around **₦11.6**, close to **short‑term resistance ~₦11.85–ₑ12**, R:R for new longs **is not ideal**.[web:295][web:240]  
- **Recommendation (0–3 days):**
  - If you are **flat**:  
    - **Do not open large new longs right below resistance**.  
    - Wait either for:
      - A **dip to support**, or  
      - A **clean breakout** above resistance.  
  - If you are **already long from lower levels**:  
    - Consider **holding** or taking **partial profit** into any push toward **₦12–ₑ13**.

#### Next 7 Days – Primary Swing Setup (Dip‑Buy)

**Scenario:** Price pulls back from ₦11–ₑ12 toward first support.

- **Primary dip‑buy zone:** **₦10.1–ₑ10.5** (short/mid support band).[web:295]  
- **Entry:**  
  - Look for **bullish daily candle** (hammer, bullish engulfing) around ₦10.2–ₑ10.5.  
- **Stop‑loss:**  
  - Below **₦9.6–ₑ9.8**, under support and recent swing lows.  
- **Targets:**  
  - **TP1:** Return to **₦11.8–ₑ12.0** (previous resistance).  
  - **TP2:** Extension toward **₦13.5–ₑ14.0**, if momentum resumes.  

**R:R (illustrative):**

- Entry E ≈ ₦10.4, Stop S ≈ ₦9.7 → Risk ≈ ₦0.7.  
- To TP1 ₦12.0 → Reward ≈ ₦1.6 (R:R ≈ 1:2.3).  
- To TP2 ₦13.8 → Reward ≈ ₦3.4 (R:R ≈ 1:4.8).  

If price **does not** dip and instead **breaks above ~₦12–ₑ12.2** on strong volume, a **breakout trade** is possible, but risk is higher as you are nearer to the ₦14.50 high.

#### Next 14–30 Days – Deeper Swing Setup

**Scenario:** Broader market pullback or FCMB‑specific correction.

- **Deeper buy zone:** **₦9.0–ₑ9.5**.  
  - Aligns with earlier consolidation and near 50‑/200‑day MAs (~₦9.8–ₑ9.9 recent).[web:272]  
- **Entry:**  
  - Wait for **reversal candles** and signs that support is holding (reduced selling volume).

This deeper setup offers **very strong R:R** but may be lower probability unless there is a significant market‑wide correction.

## 10. Technical Analysis Checklist (Pre‑Trade)

Before entering any FCMB trade, confirm:

- [ ] **HTF trend** is up or at least not sharply down.  
- [ ] Price is **near a mapped support or breakout level** (not in the middle of nowhere).  
- [ ] **RSI** is not at extreme >80 without clear continuation setup.  
- [ ] **Volume** supports the move (above average on breakouts, diminishing on pullbacks).  
- [ ] Clear **candle/pattern** (reversal, continuation, or breakout) is visible.  
- [ ] **Entry, stop and target** are defined with **R:R ≥ 1:2**.  
- [ ] Trade size respects **1–2% max account risk**.  
- [ ] Upcoming **news/earnings** are known; you are not blindly trading into binary events.
