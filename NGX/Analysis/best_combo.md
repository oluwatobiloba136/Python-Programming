## 6. Mini Position Sizing Note (Per Buy)

For each new buy, you can use:

- **Account size:** A = ₦10,000,000  
- **Risk per trade:** r% (e.g., 1–2)  
- **Entry price:** E (₦)  
- **Stop‑loss:** S (₦)

**Formulas:**

- Risk per share = E − S  
- Total risk budget = A × (r / 100)  
- Position size (shares) = (A × r / 100) ÷ (E − S)

**Example (GTCO):**

- A = ₦10,000,000  
- r = 1% → Total risk budget = ₦100,000  
- E = ₦130, S = ₦115 → Risk/share = ₦15  
- Position size = 100,000 ÷ 15 ≈ **6,600 shares** (≈ ₦858,000 position)

You can scale up if you’re comfortable taking more than 1% risk on each core name.
