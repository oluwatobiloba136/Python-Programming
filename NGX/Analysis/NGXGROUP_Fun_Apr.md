## 1. Business Model & Qualitative Overview

### 1.1 Core Business & Revenue Model

- **Company:** Nigerian Exchange Group Plc (NGX Group) – listed on NGX, ISIN **NGNGXGROUP09**, ticker **NGXGROUP**.[web:482]  
- **Activities:** Operates Nigeria’s main securities exchange infrastructure:  
  - **Listing & trading** of equities, fixed income and other securities.  
  - **Market data solutions**, indices and information services.  
  - **Licensing** of indices and related IP.  
  - **Treasury investment income** on its own financial assets.[web:486][web:495]  
- **Revenue mix (FY 2024):** Transaction fees ≈47%, listing fees ≈32%, treasury investment ≈18%, with rental/other fees <3%.[web:486]  
- **Business nature:** Largely **recurring and volume‑linked** – earnings scale with market capitalization, trading activity, listings and interest rates.

**Implication:** NGXGROUP is a **leveraged play on the Nigerian capital market cycle**; when volumes, listings and asset prices rise, revenue and profit can grow faster than GDP.

### 1.2 Industry & Competitive Landscape

- **Industry:** Stock exchange & market infrastructure within Nigeria – effectively a **regulated oligopoly / near‑monopoly** for listed equities.  
- **Competitive position:**  
  - Dominant platform for NGX‑listed equities; alternative trading venues are minimal in equity space.[web:482][web:460]  
  - Competes indirectly with **global markets** for capital allocation and with **over‑the‑counter (OTC)** markets for some instruments.  
- **Moat elements:**  
  - **Network effects:** Issuers want to list where investors are; investors go where listings and liquidity are.  
  - **Regulatory moat:** Licensed national exchange, high barriers to entry.  
  - **Brand & trust:** Long operating history (origin 1960s) and central role in Nigeria’s financial system.[web:482][web:495]

### 1.3 Leadership & Governance (High Level)

- NGXGROUP maintains standard Nigerian listed‑company governance: board, committees, published annual report & corporate governance disclosures.[web:495][web:492]  
- 2024 Annual Report notes a **board with a mix of independent and non‑executive directors**, and management that delivered a strong turnaround in profitability and cost discipline.[web:495][web:489]  
- Evidence of **insider/board shareholdings and directors’ dealings** (MarketScreener, NGX notices) supports alignment with shareholders,[web:488][web:494] though detailed quantitative ownership splits require the full annual report.

### 1.4 Growth Drivers & Key Risks

**Key growth drivers:**

- **Market activity:** FY 2024 revenue rose to ≈₦16.9bn (+103.5% YoY) primarily on higher trading activity and listing fees as NGX surpassed ₦100tn market cap in early 2026.[web:486][web:460]  
- **Listings & products:** More equity and debt listings, new ETFs/derivatives, and deeper fixed income markets.  
- **Market data & technology:** Growth in market data subscriptions, indices licensing, and digital products.  
- **Rate environment:** Higher interest rates can increase treasury investment income.

**Key risks:**

- **Market cyclicality:** Earnings are sensitive to local risk appetite, FX volatility, and macro/political shocks that affect trading volumes.  
- **Regulatory risk:** Changes in listing/fee structures, capital controls or competition policy.  
- **FX & inflation:** Naira weakness and high inflation can affect real returns and foreign participation.[web:460][web:457]

## 5. Intrinsic Value Templates (DCF & DDM)

Below are **generic templates** (fill in with your own numbers).

### 5.1 Simple FCF‑Based DCF Template (Markdown)

```markdown
## DCF Template – NGXGROUP (Illustrative)

Assumptions:
- Base year FCF: FCF₀ (e.g., ₦X bn from 2024)
- Explicit forecast period: 5–10 years
- FCF growth rate (years 1–5): g₁
- FCF growth rate (years 6–10 or terminal): g₂
- Discount rate (WACC): r
- Terminal growth rate: g_T
- Shares outstanding: N_sh

Steps:
1. Forecast FCF:
   FCF_t = FCF₀ × (1 + g₁)^t  for t = 1..5
   Then either:
   - Continue with g₂ for t = 6..10, or
   - Move directly to terminal value after year 5.

2. Discount FCFs:
   PV_FCF_t = FCF_t / (1 + r)^t

3. Terminal value (after year n):
   TV_n = FCF_n × (1 + g_T) / (r − g_T)

4. Present value of terminal value:
   PV_TV = TV_n / (1 + r)^n

5. Enterprise value (EV):
   EV = Σ PV_FCF_t + PV_TV

6. Equity value:
   Equity_Value = EV − Net_Debt   (NGXGROUP net debt likely low)

7. Intrinsic value per share:
   V₀ = Equity_Value / N_sh

Sensitivity:
- Vary g₁, g_T, and r to create optimistic / base / pessimistic scenarios.
```

### 5.2 Simple Dividend Discount Model (DDM) Template

Given NGXGROUP has **low current yield but growing earnings**, you can model dividends with growth.

```markdown
## DDM Template – NGXGROUP

Inputs:
- Current annual DPS: D₀ (e.g., ₦0.30–0.75)
- Short‑term dividend growth (next 5 years): g_D1
- Long‑term dividend growth (beyond year 5): g_D2
- Required return (cost of equity): r
- Horizon: 5 years + terminal

1. Forecast dividends:
   D_t = D₀ × (1 + g_D1)^t  for t = 1..5

2. Terminal dividend:
   D_6 = D_5 × (1 + g_D2)

3. Terminal value at t=5:
   TV_5 = D_6 / (r − g_D2)

4. Present value:
   PV = Σ [D_t / (1 + r)^t] + TV_5 / (1 + r)^5

5. Compare PV to current price P₀:
   - If PV >> P₀ → potentially undervalued.
   - If PV ≈ P₀ → fairly valued.
   - If PV << P₀ → potentially overvalued.
```

## 9. Glossary of Key Abbreviations

- **NGX:** Nigerian Exchange Limited – Nigeria’s main stock exchange. Indicates where the stock is listed.  
- **NGXGROUP:** Nigerian Exchange Group Plc – holding company of NGX; ticker for the stock.  
- **ISIN:** International Securities Identification Number – unique code for the security (here: NGNGXGROUP09).  
- **PBT:** Profit Before Tax – earnings before tax expenses; shows operating performance before tax.  
- **PAT:** Profit After Tax – net profit attributable to shareholders; used for EPS and ROE.  
- **EPS:** Earnings Per Share – PAT divided by number of shares; core driver of valuation.  
- **ROE:** Return on Equity – PAT / shareholders’ equity; measures how efficiently equity generates profit. High ROE usually indicates strong profitability.  
- **ROA:** Return on Assets – Net income / total assets; shows how efficiently assets are used to generate profit.  
- **P/E:** Price‑to‑Earnings ratio – share price divided by EPS; high P/E often implies high growth expectations.  
- **P/B:** Price‑to‑Book ratio – share price divided by book value per share; high P/B can indicate strong ROE or overvaluation.  
- **DPS:** Dividend Per Share – total cash dividends per share; relevant for income‑focused investors.  
- **Dividend Yield:** DPS / share price; annual cash return as a % of price.  
- **DCF:** Discounted Cash Flow – valuation method based on present value of future cash flows.  
- **DDM:** Dividend Discount Model – valuation method based on present value of future dividends.  
- **WACC:** Weighted Average Cost of Capital – blended cost of equity and debt used as discount rate in DCF.  
- **FCF:** Free Cash Flow – cash generated by operations after capital expenditures; main input for DCF.  
- **Beta:** Measure of stock volatility relative to the market; >1 implies higher volatility than the market.
