## 11. Glossary of Abbreviations & What They Indicate

- **HTF (Higher Timeframe)** – Weekly/monthly charts; define **primary trend**.  
- **MTF (Middle Timeframe)** – Daily charts; used for **setups and swing entries**.  
- **LTF (Lower Timeframe)** – 4h/1h/15m; refine **entry timing** and micro‑patterns.  
- **MA (Moving Average)** – Average price over N periods; price above rising MAs = bullish trend.  
- **EMA (Exponential Moving Average)** – MA with more weight on recent data; reacts faster.  
- **SMA (Simple Moving Average)** – Equal‑weighted MA; smoother but slower.  
- **RSI (Relative Strength Index)** – Momentum oscillator:  
  - >70 = overbought, <30 = oversold, 40–60 = trend zone.  
- **MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)** – Trend/momentum indicator built from EMAs:  
  - Above zero = bullish bias; crossovers show shifts.  
- **ADX (Average Directional Index)** – Trend strength gauge:  
  - >25 = strong trend, <20 = weak/sideways.  
- **BB (Bollinger Bands)** – 20‑day MA ± 2σ; upper‑band riding = strong momentum; squeezes = low‑volatility break setups.  
- **ATR (Average True Range)** – Average trading range; used to size **stops**.  
- **S/R (Support/Resistance)** – Price levels where price repeatedly bounces/stalls; key for entries, stops, targets.  
- **OBV (On‑Balance Volume)** – Cumulative up/down volume; confirms or questions price moves.  
- **VWAP (Volume‑Weighted Average Price)** – Intraday benchmark used by institutions; price above VWAP = intraday bullish bias.  
- **R:R (Risk‑Reward Ratio)** – Reward ÷ risk; higher R:R (≥1:2) improves trade expectancy.  
- **SL (Stop‑Loss)** – Pre‑defined exit to cap downside risk.  
- **TP (Take‑Profit)** – Pre‑defined level(s) where profits are realised.
