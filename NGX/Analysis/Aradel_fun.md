## 13. Guidance for Long‑Term Investors

**Overall stance:** **Buy / Accumulate**, with risk‑aware sizing.

### 13.1 Why Buy/Accumulate

- Strong business quality (high margins, high ROE/ROCE, positive FCF).  
- Attractive, sustainable dividend yield (~5–6 %).  
- Conservative leverage and solid liquidity.  
- Valuation multiples that are low versus quality and growth.

### 13.2 When to Buy (Investor Timing Logic)

1. **Valuation Levels**

   - Prefer entry/add points when:  
     - P/E is in single digits (roughly where the stock sits in the August‑2025 snapshot).  
     - EV/EBITDA is ≤6x.  
   - Be more cautious if an aggressive re‑rating pushes P/E well above mid‑teens without matching EPS growth.

2. **Price vs Range**

   - Accumulate near the lower half of the recent 52‑week range, or after multi‑week corrections driven by market risk‑off rather than company‑specific deterioration.

3. **Event‑Driven Adds**

   - Add after strong quarterly or annual results that confirm margins and growth but are followed by muted price reactions.  
   - Consider adding on over‑done sell‑offs caused by short‑term macro headlines if fundamentals remain intact.

### 13.3 When to Hold

- If you bought lower and:  
  - Fundamentals remain strong (margins, ROE, FCF).  
  - P/E has moved up but remains at or below a mid‑teens level.  
- In this scenario, holding for continued compounding and dividend income is rational.

### 13.4 When to Consider Trimming or Selling

- Multiple signs of fundamental deterioration:  
  - Sustained margin compression.  
  - ROE and ROCE trending down meaningfully.  
  - FCF turning structurally negative.  
  - Debt and D/E rising sharply without clearly high‑return projects.  
  - Repeated governance issues (multiple filing delays, weak disclosure).  
- Or if the stock re‑rates to **rich** levels (e.g., P/E 18–20x+) while growth slows.

## 14. Guidance for Short‑Term Traders

**Overall stance:** **Tactically Bullish**, but trade with respect for volatility.

### 14.1 How to Trade Aradel

1. **Buy‑the‑Dip in an Uptrend**

   - Use the 52‑week range, moving averages and key support zones to identify pullbacks in a broader uptrend.  
   - Confirm that there is no new fundamental or governance shock before entering.

2. **Earnings and Dividend Catalysts**

   - Trade around results and dividend dates:  
     - Go long on clean beats and strong margin commentary with breakout price action.  
     - Avoid or reduce exposure if guidance or margins disappoint, regardless of headline EPS.

3. **Risk Management**

   - Keep position sizes moderate due to the wide daily ranges and gap risk.  
   - Use stop‑losses below recent support levels and adjust for volatility.

4. **When Not to Trade Long**

   - Ahead of known high‑risk macro events (major FX policy changes, high‑stakes elections) where country risk can dominate stock‑specific fundamentals.  
   - When there are unresolved or recurring governance issues (e.g., repeated filing delays).
