## Glossary of Abbreviations & What They Indicate

- **HTF (Higher Timeframe)** – Weekly/monthly charts. Indicates **primary trend**; trade direction should align with HTF.  
- **MTF (Middle Timeframe)** – Daily charts. Used for **setup & entries** within the HTF trend.  
- **LTF (Lower Timeframe)** – 4h/1h/15m. Used for **fine entry timing** and micro‑patterns.  
- **MA (Moving Average)** – Average price over N periods (e.g. 20, 50, 200).  
  - Price above rising MAs = **uptrend**; below falling MAs = **downtrend**.  
- **EMA (Exponential Moving Average)** – MA that **weights recent prices more**; reacts faster than SMA.  
- **SMA (Simple Moving Average)** – Equal‑weighted moving average; smoother but slower than EMA.  
- **RSI (Relative Strength Index)** – Momentum oscillator (0–100).  
  - >70 = **overbought**, <30 = **oversold**; 40–60 = **neutral trend zone**.  
- **MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)** – Trend oscillator from EMAs.  
  - Above zero = bullish bias; crossovers and histogram changes indicate **momentum shifts**.  
- **ADX (Average Directional Index)** – Measure of **trend strength** (0–100).  
  - <20 = weak/no trend; >25 = trending; >40 often = very strong trend.  
- **BB (Bollinger Bands)** – 20‑day SMA ± 2 standard deviations; price hugging upper band = **strong uptrend/overextension**; “squeeze” = low volatility before breakout.  
- **ATR (Average True Range)** – Average range per bar; used to set **volatility‑adjusted stops**. Higher ATR means wider stops needed.  
- **S/R (Support/Resistance)** – Key price levels where price has repeatedly bounced (support) or stalled (resistance). Critical for entries, exits, and stop placement.  
- **Cup‑and‑Handle** – Bullish continuation pattern; rounded base (cup) followed by a small consolidation (handle). Breakout above **neckline** often leads to strong upside.  
- **OBV (On‑Balance Volume)** – Cumulative volume flow; rising OBV confirms buying pressure.  
- **VWAP (Volume‑Weighted Average Price)** – Intraday average price weighted by volume; large players often reference VWAP.  
- **R:R (Risk‑Reward ratio)** – Reward / risk per trade; values **≥1:2** preferred.  
- **SL (Stop‑Loss)** – Level where trade is exited to limit loss.  
- **TP (Take Profit)** – Target level(s) to lock in gains.
