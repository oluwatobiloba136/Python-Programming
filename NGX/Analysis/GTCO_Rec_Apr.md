## 6. Simple Position‑Sizing Template (Markdown)

```markdown
### Position Sizing for GTCO Trade

Inputs:
- Account size: A (₦)
- Max risk per trade: r%  (e.g. 1–2%)
- Entry price: E (₦)
- Stop-loss: S (₦)

Risk per share = E − S
Total risk allowed = A × r

Position size (shares) = (A × r) ÷ (E − S)

Example:
- A = ₦5,000,000
- r = 1% → total risk = ₦50,000
- E = ₦122
- S = ₦114

Risk per share = 122 − 114 = ₦8
Position size = 50,000 ÷ 8 = 6,250 shares
```
