## 8. Glossary of Technical Abbreviations

- **ASI:** All‑Share Index (NGX’s broad market index). Shows overall market trend and risk appetite.[web:497]  
- **MA (Moving Average):** Average price over a set period (e.g., 50‑day, 200‑day). Price far above MA implies extension.  
- **RSI (Relative Strength Index):** Momentum oscillator (0–100). >70 often overbought; <30 oversold.  
- **MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence):** Trend‑following indicator; crossovers and histogram show momentum shifts.  
- **ADX (Average Directional Index):** Measures trend strength. >25 = strong trend; <20 = weak.  
- **ATR (Average True Range):** Volatility measure; higher ATR means wider typical daily swings and wider stops.  
- **OBV (On‑Balance Volume):** Cumulative volume indicator; rising OBV confirms accumulation, falling OBV confirms distribution.  
- **Support:** Price area where demand tends to appear and halt declines.  
- **Resistance:** Price area where supply tends to appear and cap advances.  
- **Distribution:** Phase where smart money sells into rising prices and thinning volume; often precedes tops.  
- **Blow‑off top:** Rapid vertical price spike with extreme volume and then sharp reversal.  
- **Pullback:** Short‑term decline within an overall uptrend; often used as entry opportunity.  
- **Breakout:** Price move above a key resistance level, ideally with strong volume.
