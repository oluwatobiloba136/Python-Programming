## 10. Glossary of Technical Terms

- **RSI (Relative Strength Index):** Momentum oscillator; >70 = overbought, <30 = oversold; mid‑range 40–60 = neutral trend zone.  
- **MACD:** Trend‑following indicator based on moving averages; crossovers and histogram shifts can signal momentum changes.  
- **ADX:** Average Directional Index; measures trend strength (>25 strong, <20 weak).  
- **ATR (Average True Range):** Volatility gauge; helps set volatility‑adjusted stops.  
- **Moving Average (MA):** Average price over a period; used to define trend and dynamic support/resistance.  
- **Support / Resistance:** Price levels where demand or supply has historically been strong enough to reverse or stall price.  
- **Breakout:** Price move beyond a significant support/resistance level, ideally on strong volume.  
- **Pullback:** Short‑term move against the trend; buying pullbacks in an uptrend is lower‑risk than chasing highs.
